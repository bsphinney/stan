Curio Cabinet

Oldest successful ID

January 26, 2006 — LTQ ion trap

File LTQ20060126bsa — the earliest BSA injection in the archive with confident identifications. The LTQ-only Sage search (wider fragment tolerance, no high-res MS2) found 184 BSA PSMs at 1% FDR, covering 34.3% of the 607-AA bovine albumin sequence. The Orbitrap era was still six years away.

184 PSMs · 34.3% coverage
All-time record

May 3, 2017 — Q-Exactive Plus

File qeP_20170503_BSA_oldlot1 — 921 BSA PSMs at 1% FDR, the highest in the 17-year archive. 56.0% sequence coverage: 40 unique BSA peptides from a single 60-min LC-MS run. This is also from the "old lot 1" BSA transition — the jump in PSMs partly reflects both instrument maturity and a fresh reagent lot.

921 PSMs · 56.0% coverage
The Michrom Era

July 7, 2008 — Michrom HPLC

Five injections in the archive are explicitly labelled Michrome — the Michrom Bioresources HPLC system that the UC Davis Proteomics Core used in the mid-2000s before switching to EasyLC / Dionex systems. These runs predate nano-flow standardization and produced 0 confident PSMs with modern Sage parameters — a reminder of how much LC improvements contributed to the sensitivity gains of the Orbitrap era.

5 runs · 0 PSMs (modern params)
The BSA Lot Switch

2017 — When the reagent changed

Two files in the archive carry the oldlot1 suffix — a deliberate documentation of a BSA reagent lot transition. Both runs used the same Q-Exactive Plus; both are among the top-5 all-time PSM scores. Comparing injections across lot transitions is exactly the kind of traceability that a 17-year QC archive enables. Neither lot produced a detectable difference in coverage (56.0% vs 52.7%).

May 2017 vs Dec 2017 · ~3.3% Δ coverage
LTQ-FT peak

Aug 25, 2006 — the hybrid advantage

File ft082506bsa-nr achieved 534 BSA PSMs on the LTQ-FT — the highest for any hybrid FT instrument in the archive, and competitive with early Orbitrap results half a decade later. The LTQ-FT's Fourier-transform analyzer gave it a decisive edge over pure ion-trap instruments even at 2006 scan speeds.

534 PSMs · 26.2% coverage
2022 coda

May 2022 — last BSA entry

The archive's newest entry, bsa2_081120220505, dates from May 2022. By this point the lab had transitioned to HeLa digest as the community-standard QC sample — the reason today's STAN pipeline monitors HeLa rather than BSA. The BSA era formally ended as STAN began. The two corpora now overlap in this museum.

2005 → 2022 → HeLa → ∞
